## 1. Baseline refusal is a binary/saturated detector at tested intensity

The refusal dose curve was:

```text
explicit refusal occurrences: 0, 1, 2, 4

occurrence candidate: 0.0, 1.0, 2.0, 4.0
baseline R_mean:      0.0, 1.0, 1.0, 1.0
```

The baseline distinguished absence from presence, but it did not distinguish one from two or four refusals.

Plateaus:

```text
refusal-dose-1 -> refusal-dose-2
refusal-dose-2 -> refusal-dose-4
```

**Pre-canon consequence:** baseline `R_mean` may remain a refusal-presence candidate, but it cannot be treated as an occurrence, dose, persistence, or accumulation measure.

The occurrence candidate and occurrence-weighted UCNS cell-detail breadth both rose strictly across the four cases:

```text

UCNS occurrence-turn B.cell-detail:
3.3862943611, 3.7917594692, 5.8903717579, 10.0875963352
```

These are candidate-specific orderings, not canonization.

## 2. Baseline constraint coverage failed all four families

The baseline `C_mean` produced zero for both high and low cases in every tested family:

```text
known must/no-alternative: 0.0 vs 0.0
only-option/deadline:      0.0 vs 0.0
consequence:               0.0 vs 0.0
mandatory-authority:       0.0 vs 0.0
```

This extends the v0.1 marker-coverage falsifier. The failure is broader than one holdout phrase.

**Pre-canon consequence:** the frozen baseline `C` axis cannot presently serve as the sole constraint-pressure measure. Integrity of its frozen files establishes reproducibility, not coverage.

## 3. The occurrence-and-coverage candidate detected all high/low contrasts

Candidate high versus low occurrence values were:

```text
known:       3.0 vs 0.0
option:      2.0 vs 0.0
consequence: 2.0 vs 1.0
authority:   1.0 vs 0.0
```

Three controls were clean. The consequence control was not.

The low-control sentence contained:

```text
without penalty
```

The candidate counted `penalty` without interpreting the negating scope of `without`.

**New falsifier:** phrase occurrence is not semantic assertion. A candidate that ignores local negation can report pressure where the text explicitly denies it.

**Pre-canon consequence:** EDCM requires an explicit assertion/scope layer between lexical evidence and an operating constraint readout. Negated, quoted, hypothetical, conditional, attributed, and repaired language must remain distinguishable.

The present candidate is therefore useful evidence machinery but not a phrase-robust canon candidate.


Terminal tension was also only partially ordered:


The baseline preserved strict latency order through both tested summaries:

```text

0.2183312652 < 0.2569092344 < 0.3326169795

baseline kappa_final:
0.0000000000 < 0.0456594994 < 0.2453148611
```

**Pre-canon consequence:** horizon, peak, accumulated, and terminal tension are separate readouts. None may silently replace the others. The baseline circuit dynamics retain useful latency behavior even though baseline `R` and `C` failed their dose and coverage tests.

## 5. UCNS support assignment changes the answer

Four support policies were compared with the same UCNS `cell-detail` breadth candidate.

### Unit-turn

Unit support collapsed every equal-turn-count high/low constraint pair:

```text
known:     equal
option:    equal
consequence: equal
authority: equal
```

This is declared blindness, not a software error.

### Token-turn

Token support reversed two intended pressure rankings because the lower-pressure text was longer:

```text
known high:       5.2958368660
known low:        5.6888794541

consequence high: 5.4657359028
consequence low:  5.5553480615
```

**Pre-canon consequence:** textual extent is not constraint pressure. Token support may be useful for retained textual quantity but cannot stand in as a pressure assignment.

### Occurrence-turn and dissonance-turn

Both preserved all declared high/low and refusal-dose orderings in this corpus.

This is experiment-supported evidence only. Their phrase counts inherit the negation problem described above.

## 6. Structural and measurement consequences

The cumulative v0.1 and v0.2 evidence supports carrying these restrictions forward:

1. preserve exact order until a readout-specific policy explicitly discards it;
2. preserve multiplicity even when a set view is also retained;
3. separate refusal presence from refusal occurrence and dose;
4. separate lexical phrase evidence from asserted constraint state;
5. retain negation and scope before converting text into support or pressure;
6. separate resolution horizon, peak, accumulated, and terminal tension;
7. keep support assignment explicit and versioned;
8. do not treat token length as constraint intensity;
9. keep baseline circuit dynamics as a viable latency candidate despite failures in baseline `R` and `C`;
10. preserve v0.1 and v0.2 falsifiers as permanent evidence.

## Next experiment boundary

Version 0.3 should pressure assertion and scope rather than merely adding synonyms:

- asserted versus negated pressure;
- direct versus quoted pressure;
- actual versus hypothetical or conditional pressure;
- speaker-owned versus attributed refusal;
- local repair versus later contradiction;
- identical phrase counts under different scope topology.

This creates the next reciprocal test: EDCM proposes scope-bearing event distinctions; UCNS must preserve the relations required to keep those distinctions from collapsing.

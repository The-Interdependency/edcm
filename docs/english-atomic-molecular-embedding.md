# English Atomic and Molecular Gonol Embeddings

Status: **first executable foundation**.

This document freezes the doctrine selected by Erin Spencer for the first
English embedding run. The run is bounded to **Open English WordNet 2025** for
attestation, senses, relations, and soundness evidence. The construction law is
not bounded by dictionary convention.

## One composition operation at every scale

Affix-to-root composition is the same transformation as word-to-phrase
composition. Affixes, roots, words, phrases, sentences, and paragraphs are
gonols occupying different relational scales.

A gonol is molecular relative to its constituents and atomic relative to the
larger gonol in which it participates.

The implementation therefore exposes one composer. Convenience functions may
later speak of words, phrases, or sentences, but they must delegate to that
same composer and must not introduce scale-specific algebra.

Explicit grouping remains metadata even where the currently maintained local
UCNS product is associative. No grouping distinction may be silently deleted
from the molecular record merely because two current intrinsic results compare
equivalent.

## Universal composition

Every declared affix can and must compose with every declared root, compound,
and prior molecular result. Selection restrictions are descriptive evidence
about conventional use, never construction gates.

A generated expression can be:

- valid and sound;
- valid and unsound;
- valid and unattested;
- valid and unconventional;
- valid and presently technology-pending.

`ilproperlies` is the canonical small fixture:

- structurally valid;
- absent from the selected dictionary boundary unless evidence says otherwise;
- currently unsound within this run;
- retained rather than rejected.

Soundness is evidentiary and may later need to be indexed by context,
technology, community, or time.

## The true fork

### Direct atomic embedding

Each dictionary word is assigned a whole-word gonol independently from its
molecular decomposition. The eventual assignment law must use whole-word
Open English WordNet senses and relations without borrowing the molecular
result as an initial placement.

### Molecular embedding

Roots, affixes, compounds, and prior molecular results are composed with the
one universal UCNS product. The full ordered and grouped composition tree is
preserved outside the intrinsic gonol.

### Generated atomic view

A molecular result can participate as one atomic gonol at the next scale. It
may be calculated on demand or cached. Lazy versus eager materialization is not
a fork when the canonical identity remains identical.

The fork is real only between:

1. the independently assigned direct atomic gonol; and
2. the atomic view generated from molecular composition.

Their equality, divergence, partial correspondence, lexicalization, opacity,
irregularity, and drift are observations rather than assumptions.

## Source-data boundary

The repository does not currently vendor the complete Open English WordNet
2025 release. This foundation therefore does not claim that the affix list,
root list, sense graph, or direct atomic assignment is complete.

The next data-scale change must:

- pin the exact Open English WordNet 2025 release artifact and digest;
- preserve its license and attribution;
- declare the accepted source format;
- extract affixes first, roots second, and transformations/renderings third;
- distinguish dictionary-attested records from universally valid generated
  records;
- avoid consulting other dictionaries during this run;
- produce repeatable counts and rejected-source diagnostics;
- never infer soundness merely from successful composition.

## Metadata-free gonol lists

Intrinsic artifacts contain only canonical UCNS object data:

- declared carrier;
- minimal carrier;
- ordered anchors;
- recursive payloads;
- face states.

They contain no:

- words or labels;
- dictionary definitions or sense ids;
- attestation or soundness status;
- source or provenance fields;
- direct, generated, atomic, or molecular classifications.

The metadata-bearing index is a separate artifact. Positional order and file
digests bind that index to the clean list without contaminating the intrinsic
records.

The initial implementation can emit separately frozen lists for:

- direct atomic gonols;
- molecular gonols;
- generated atomic views.

Whether the molecular object and generated atomic view require separate files
will be decided by whether crossing scale contributes intrinsic structure. If
it does not, a shared canonical identity and separate metadata indexes are
preferable.

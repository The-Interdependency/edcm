relations and closure rules. Consume UCNS constructors and geometry. Preserve
identity, order, multiplicity, occurrence positions and provenance. Once closed,
a gonol participates atomically at an admissible consuming scale; no universal
mandatory adjacent-scale ladder is imposed. Pronunciation participates only if
the owning construction explicitly admits it.

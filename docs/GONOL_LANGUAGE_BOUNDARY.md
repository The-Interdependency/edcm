## Fixed language-object ladder

For the current stack language path, the objects are gonols throughout:

```text
Unicode character gonols
        ↓
fixed floor word gonols
        ↓
complete lexical floor F
        ↓
definitions expressed only with members of F
        ↓
floor-definition gonols
```

Unicode character gonols are the primitive inscription objects. A floor word is
not a tokenizer token or an opaque identifier: it is a gonol constructed from
its exact ordered character-gonol inscription under UCNS-owned rules.

Once the lexical floor `F` is admitted, it is closed. A semantic definition of
a floor word may not enlarge that floor. Every floor-definition gonol must
satisfy the upstream closure condition:

```text
support(definition_gonol(word, sense)) ⊆ F
```

A definition requiring lexical material outside `F` is not a floor definition.
It does not admit the missing word, recursively expand the vocabulary, or create
an exception to closure.

## No hidden token or vector layer

The stack language path does not insert conventional NLP tokens, token IDs,
subword pieces, or an opaque embedding lookup between inscription and gonol
construction.

Source prose may be retained as provenance and custody evidence. Arbitrary text
is not thereby the semantic representation.

A floor-definition gonol is intended to occupy the semantic-representation role
for which conventional systems commonly use vector embeddings: the word is
represented relationally through already-known lexical gonols rather than by an
opaque coordinate vector. This is a role analogy only. It establishes no
semantic quality, similarity behavior, benchmark advantage, or downstream
utility.

## EDCM ownership and use

EDCM does **not** own:

- lexical-floor membership;
- Unicode-character-gonol identity or construction;
- the UCNS word-gonol composition law;
- the UCNS definition-gonol composition law; or
- semantic validity merely because a UCNS representation exists.

EDCM may:

- consume exact source-bound UCNS gonol identities and receipts;
- preserve order, multiplicity, sense identity, context, source, and provenance;
- construct measurement candidates over those represented trajectories when an
  explicit EDCM mapping is declared;
- compare a definition-gonol representation against simpler baselines or
  conventional embeddings under preregistered criteria; and
- record `FALSIFIED`, `SURVIVED` — not proved, `UNRESOLVED`, or `BLOCKED`
  outcomes without changing the upstream construction after outcome inspection.

UCNS representation does not activate EDCM measurement. EDCM measurement does
not validate UCNS semantics or prove the UCNS construction.

None of those surfaces defines the current stack lexical primitive. Historical

gonols merely because EDCM can ingest them.

The old broad statement that “higher-gonol composition remains unresolved” is
too coarse for the current boundary. Gonol composition already exists at
multiple declared language grains. What remains unresolved here is narrower:
the exact UCNS composition/relation law and source-custody process for producing
a complete set of **closed floor-definition gonols**, followed by an explicit
EDCM consumer/evaluation contract if measurement is desired.

## Usage guidance

For reproduction of historical EDCM artifacts, continue to use the exact pinned
producer and profile named by those artifacts. Do not rewrite historical reports
or identifiers to match this later language boundary.

For new semantic work:

1. consume the fixed lexical-floor identity from UCNS;
2. require definitions whose lexical support is entirely inside that floor;
3. require source-bound, replayable definition-gonol receipts from UCNS;
4. reject hidden tokenizer, subword, or opaque-vector substitution;
5. keep arbitrary source definition prose as provenance rather than semantic
   representation;
6. declare any EDCM projection or metric separately; and
7. evaluate the representation only after the construction and criteria are
   frozen.
